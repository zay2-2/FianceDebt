Your Roadmap
01
Job Searching
▼
  • Make minimum payments only — preserve every dollar while unemployed
  • Any freelance or gig income goes straight to the highest-APR debt first
  • Audit every subscription and cancel anything non-essential today
  • Track applications in a spreadsheet — 5 quality apps/week beats 50 sprays
  • When you get an offer, negotiate — even $5k more is hundreds toward debt
  • Ask about signing bonuses; entry-level tech roles sometimes offer $5–15k
02
First Paycheck
▼
  • Set up an auto-transfer on payday — pay debt before you see the money
  • Update your salary here and lock in the required monthly payment amount
  • Build a $1,000 emergency fund first so unexpected costs don't spiral debt
  • Cancel any trials you signed up for during the job search
  • Optimize your W-4 withholdings to avoid a surprise tax bill in April
  • Start tracking net worth monthly — watching it rise is genuinely motivating
03
Final Push
▼
  • Every tax refund, bonus, and windfall goes directly to remaining balance
  • Avalanche method: once a debt hits $0, roll its payment to the next one
  • Set milestones at 25%, 50%, 75% — small free rewards keep you going
  • Cut all discretionary for the final 90 days if the finish line is close
  • Visualize a specific day in December as your debt-free date — make it real
  • Decide now what you'll do with that freed monthly cash flow afterward
